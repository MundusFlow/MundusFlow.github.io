GETTING STARTED

How do I download my purchase?

If you bought through Stripe, your download link is in the receipt email sent right after checkout. If you bought through Etsy, go to Etsy → Purchases and Reviews to download your file. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

How do I open an Excel file?

Every file is a standard .xlsx spreadsheet. Open it with Microsoft Excel, or upload it to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets — no special software required.

How do I use a Google Sheets template?

Upload the downloaded .xlsx file to Google Drive, then open it — Drive converts it to a Google Sheet automatically. From there it works like any other Google Sheet.

Where do I find the free tools?

Every free calculator is listed on our Free Tools page, and you'll also find the ones for your trade on each product's page.

USING OUR PRODUCTS

How do I enter my information?

Look for the blue cells — those are the ones you're meant to fill in. Everything else calculates automatically, so you don't need to touch it.

What do the calculations mean?

Each workbook includes a "How to Use" tab that walks through what every number means and where it comes from.

Can I customize the spreadsheet?

Yes. These are standard, unlocked Excel/Google Sheets files — you're free to edit labels, colors, and formulas to fit how you work.

Can I use the files for my business?

Yes — once purchased, the file is yours to use for your own business for as long as you like.

ORDERS & PURCHASES

Where is my download?

Check the receipt/confirmation email from Stripe, or your Purchases and Reviews page on Etsy. If it's genuinely missing, contact us below and we'll help you get it.

I purchased the wrong product

Contact us with your order details and which product you meant to get — we'll help sort it out.

This is almost always a spam-folder or delivery-platform issue. Contact us with your order number and we'll get the file to you directly.

Can I get a refund?

Reach out and tell us what happened — refund requests are handled case by case, and digital purchases through Etsy or Stripe follow that platform's own purchase protections as well.

TECHNICAL HELP

The spreadsheet isn't calculating correctly

Double-check that a formula cell wasn't accidentally overwritten with a typed value. If something still looks wrong, re-download a fresh copy of the file and compare.

A file won't open

Make sure it downloaded fully and isn't still zipped. If you're on a phone, try opening it from a laptop or desktop spreadsheet app first.

Google Sheets formatting looks different

That's normal — Google Sheets doesn't render every Excel formatting feature identically. The formulas and data are unaffected.

I found an error in a tool

Please let us know exactly what you found and where — we take these seriously and want every tool to be accurate.
